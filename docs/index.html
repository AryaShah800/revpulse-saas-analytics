RevPulse: where simulated SaaS revenue leaks

B2B SaaS revenue, billing and retention analytics. ALL DATA IS SIMULATED (seed 42, 1,000 accounts, 36 months to Dec 2025). Amounts in INR.

-16.7% MRR
Simulated monthly recurring revenue is INR 3,622,900, 16.7% below a year earlier. Expansion and contraction nearly cancel (net INR 9,500); churned MRR (INR 1,092,700) drives the fall.

Action: Put retention ahead of expansion: track churned MRR per month as the lead KPI.

Impact: Reactivation recovers only INR 356,200 of the INR 1,092,700 churned over 12 months. The simulation signs up no new accounts after month 18, so this says nothing about acquisition.

80.4% is 61+ days
Of INR 9,523,419 outstanding across 1,119 unpaid invoices, INR 7,658,901 (998 invoices) is more than 60 days old.

Action: Work the 61+ day invoices first; check disputed and returned payments before outreach.

Impact: The largest single pool of cash at risk, so chasing the oldest bucket recovers the most per call.

92.5% collected
Referral is the top-value channel (29.2% of MRR) but collects 92.5% of invoiced value against 94.2% overall; weakest channel: Referral.

Action: Aim collections effort at Referral-sourced accounts first.

Impact: Descriptive, not tested; recent invoices may not be due yet, so part of the gap is timing.

82.8% at month 12
82.8% of accounts are still active at month 12; 11.5 of the 17.2 points lost are gone by month 6.

Action: Focus onboarding and customer-success effort on the first six months.

Impact: Most of the retention loss happens early, so month-6 retention is the metric to move.

14.3% recall
The churn model (held-out PR-AUC 0.139 vs 0.024 prevalence) catches only 14.3% of churners; precision is 17.9%.

Action: Use the risk list as a triage aid, not a strategy; check usage decline, tickets and payment context before acting.

Impact: A weak but better-than-baseline signal on simulated data; it should not drive spend on its own.

Read with care. Synthetic data: these findings describe a generated dataset, not a real business. No new accounts are simulated after month 18. The churn model was evaluated on a chronological hold-out and is weak.
